Executive training

Digital assets and payments, taught by practitioners

Practical one-day programmes for banks and financial institutions in the UAE and GCC. Clear foundations, regional examples and a group exercise, so participants leave able to judge what matters for their own institution.

Programme 1

Digital Assets and Tokenization for UAE Finance Professionals

What is real, what is hype, and what it means for a bank.

  • From ledger to token: blockchain, keys, smart contracts, on-chain money
  • The market and its plumbing: custody, venues, where banks fit
  • Risk and rules: lessons from failures, UAE and GCC regimes, Islamic finance

Exercise: teams review a bank's proposal to launch a tokenized product and decide whether it is ready for approval.

Programme 2

Digital Wallets and the UAE's New Payment Rails

How money moves today, and how that is changing.

  • Anatomy of a payment: cards, transfers, mobile and the economics
  • The UAE's rails: national infrastructure and the CBUAE programme
  • Wallets and security: wallet models, payment tokens, fraud and mules

Exercise: teams design a new digital bank's payment offering on a limited budget and pitch the trade-offs to a board.

FormatOne day in-house, or a half-day executive briefing
Group size10 to 25, mixed or single-function teams
IncludesSlides, exercise, quizzes, pre- and post-course assessment
TailoringBuilt around your institution; Islamic finance lens on request

Also available as a 6 to 10 hour multi-session programme for academies and universities, with an assessed group pitch.
